NexusOps is a production-grade, self-hosted automation platform built on n8n (queue mode), Traefik, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Qdrant. Deployable with `make up`, it delivers encrypted age-encrypted backups with automated restore drills, LLM token/cost tracking with budget guardrails, and human-in-the-loop Telegram approvals with escalation — all as version-controlled workflows in Git. Includes CI/CD (lint, Trivy, smoke tests, restore drills), observability (`make doctor`, optional Prometheus/Grafana), and comprehensive runbooks. MIT licensed.
